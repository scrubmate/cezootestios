Refund Policy

At CEZOO, we aim to deliver products in good condition and provide a reliable shopping experience. Refund, replacement, or return eligibility depends on the product category, condition of the product, nature of the issue, and the applicable policy shown for that product.

1. Product-Specific Refund Policy

Not every product available on CEZOO is eligible for return or refund. Some products may be eligible for replacement only, while others may be eligible for a refund when the reported issue meets the applicable conditions.

Where applicable, the return, replacement, or refund conditions may be displayed on the product page, order details page, or other relevant section of the CEZOO platform.

2. Products That May Be Eligible

A refund or replacement request may be considered when an eligible product is damaged during delivery, defective, expired, materially different from the product ordered, or when an incorrect product has been delivered.

Eligibility is subject to verification of the issue and the specific policy applicable to the product category.

3. Perishable Products

Fruits and other perishable products generally have limited return eligibility because of their nature and shelf life. If a perishable product is delivered damaged, spoiled, or in an unacceptable condition, the user should report the issue as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

CEZOO may request photographs or other reasonable information to verify the condition of the product before approving a refund or replacement.

4. Grocery and Packaged Products

Packaged grocery products may be considered for refund or replacement when the delivered item is incorrect, damaged, leaking, expired, or otherwise materially defective at the time of delivery.

Products that have been substantially used, altered, damaged after delivery, or returned without original packaging where packaging is reasonably required for verification may not be eligible.

5. Gifts, Stationery and Other Products

Eligibility for gifts, stationery, household items, and other non-perishable products may vary by product. Some products may qualify for replacement or refund for manufacturing defects, transit damage, missing parts, or delivery of an incorrect item.

Change-of-mind returns are not automatically available for every product. The applicable product policy should be checked before purchase where such information is displayed.

6. Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may be marked as non-returnable because of hygiene, safety, perishability, short shelf life, or other product-specific reasons. Such products will generally not be eligible for return unless they were delivered damaged, defective, expired, or incorrect and the issue is verified.

7. Reporting an Issue

To request assistance, users should open the Help or Customer Support section and provide the relevant order information and a clear description of the issue.

CEZOO may request photographs of the product, packaging, expiry date, batch information, or other relevant evidence where reasonably necessary to review the request.

8. Refund Review

Refund and replacement requests are reviewed based on the information provided, the condition of the product, order records, delivery details, and the policy applicable to the product.

Submission of a request does not automatically guarantee approval. CEZOO will communicate the available resolution after reviewing the request.

9. Refund Method

Approved refunds for prepaid orders will generally be processed through the original payment method where supported. In some situations, another available refund method may be offered.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear may vary depending on the bank, UPI application, card issuer, payment gateway, or other payment service provider involved in the transaction.

10. Order Cancellation

An order may be cancelled only while cancellation is available for the applicable order status. Once an order has reached a stage where preparation, packing, dispatch, or delivery cannot reasonably be reversed, cancellation may no longer be available.

If CEZOO cancels a prepaid order or an unavailable item from a prepaid order, the applicable amount will be processed for refund according to the relevant payment process.

11. Missing Products

If a confirmed and paid product is missing from the delivered order, users should report the issue through CEZOO support. After verification, CEZOO may provide an appropriate refund, replacement, or other available resolution.

12. Promotional Items and Coupons

Refund calculations for discounted orders, coupons, promotional bundles, or rewards may take into account the actual amount paid and the conditions of the applicable promotion.

Coupon value, promotional benefits, or rewards may not always be refundable or restorable unless specifically stated in the applicable offer conditions.
